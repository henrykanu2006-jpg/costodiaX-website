What's included

Built for the Nigerian logistics industry.

Every module built specifically for how Nigerian clearing agents and logistics companies actually work — not adapted from generic business software.

01
Jobs & 8-stage tracking
Track every job through all 8 stages of the Nigerian clearance process — documents received, PAAR filed, vessel arrived, DO collected, duties paid, under examination, released and delivered.
02
Nigerian customs invoicing
Invoices with VAT (7.5%), CISS (1% of CIF), ETLS (0.5% of CIF) and duty advance — all calculated automatically at your CBN rate with PDF download.
03
Bonded terminal management
Log cargo, set daily rates, track storage charges from day one. Own terminal and third-party terminals tracked separately. Weekly reports generated automatically.
04
Client cargo portal
Your clients log in and see their shipments, documents and invoices in real time. Personalised greeting. No more "where is my cargo?" calls to your office.
05
Staff role management
Port runners see only their assigned jobs. Finance officers see only invoices. Drivers see their trips. Each role gets exactly the access they need.
